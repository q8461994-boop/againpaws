About This Piece

Ready to send, made to last.

A quiet, solid resting place. The Classic Square Urn is cast in a heavyweight stone-grey material with brushed gold paw prints and the words “we love you forever” along the base. A window on the front holds your pet's photo, so the urn reads as a framed picture rather than a box. Available in pink, white, black and grey.

These pieces are ready-designed, so there is no artwork or proofing step — we pack and ship within a few working days. If you would like a photo print trimmed to fit, say so when you order.

Good To Know

Common questions.

What size pet does it suit?

With 1100 ml of interior space it suits most cats and small to medium dogs. For larger pets, message us and we will advise.

Is the urn personalised?

No — the urn ships as designed, with a photo window you fill yourself.

How do I insert the photo?

Trim a print to the window size, slide it behind the front frame and press the frame back into place.
