A gentle way to keep them close

When a pet passes away, the photos that remain often say more than words. A custom needle-felted memorial gift turns one of those photos into something you can hold: the curled-up sleeping pose, the grey around the muzzle, the way one ear never stood up quite straight.

Made for older photos too

You do not need perfect studio shots. Blurry phone pictures, photos from years ago, even a favorite snapshot where only the face is sharp — send what you have. Before production starts we will tell you honestly what we can capture from your photos, so there are no surprises.

Timing, care and next steps

Production takes 2–4 weeks plus shipping, and larger memorial pieces include progress photos so you can follow along. Many families order two: one to keep, one for a parent or child. To begin, send us your photos and mention it is a memorial order — we will handle it with the extra care it deserves. You can also read the full process guide or the FAQ first.
