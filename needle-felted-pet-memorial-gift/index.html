A custom needle-felted piece is made one at a time from your references. It is not a stock portrait: your photos guide the colors, markings and expression that make the memory personal.

Preparing photos for a memorial keepsake

Start with the clearest photo you have of the face, then add a side or full-body image when available. Older photos are welcome; send more than one if a particular marking or expression is important.
