Choosing

Three questions that pick the right pet memorial gift.

For the home or the garden?

A window suncatcher and a portrait urn stay indoors; a garden stone and the wind chime live outside.

Something to hold or something to see?

Handheld pieces comfort on hard days; wall and shelf pieces keep a pet present every day. Our pet memorial gift ideas guide compares all ten options by budget.

What photos do you have?

Phone photos are enough — even old, slightly blurry ones. What makes a good photo explains exactly what our makers need.

Frequently asked about pet memorial gifts

What is a pet memorial gift?

A pet memorial gift is something given to honour a pet who has died — usually a keepsake made from the pet's own photos, such as a felted portrait, engraved wind chime, garden stone or photo urn. The point is specificity: a piece that looks like their pet, not a generic paw print.

What is the best pet memorial gift?

The one made from the pet's actual photo. A custom needle-felted portrait ($87+) is the closest likeness and the most personal option; a garden stone ($23.50) or wind chime ($39) is the most affordable way to mark a place.

How much should a pet memorial gift cost?

$20–50 covers a garden stone, urn, wind chime or suncatcher. $87–$477 buys a handmade felted portrait, priced by size and detail. Anything above that should be reserved for very close relationships.

Can a pet memorial gift be made from old photos?

Usually yes. Makers work from phone snapshots all the time; what matters is a clear view of the face and markings. Send what you have and ask what is possible before paying.

How long does a custom pet memorial gift take?

Memorial Collection pieces are made to order in about 1–2 weeks. Handmade felted portraits take longer — see how long a custom pet portrait takes for the full timeline, including shipping.
