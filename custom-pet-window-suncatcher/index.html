Good To Know

Common questions.

Which size should I choose?

15 cm suits a small kitchen window, 20 cm is the most popular for living-room windows, and 25 cm makes the portrait the focal point of a larger window.

Will the colours fade in direct sun?

The panel is made to hang in daylight. As with any printed keepsake, a spot that avoids hours of harsh afternoon sun will keep the colours truest.

Can I choose what the pet is doing?

Send several photos — sitting, lying down, head tilt — and we will recommend the pose that converts best into a round portrait.
