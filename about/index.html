About Again Paws

The hands & heart behind every keepsake.

We believe a pet is family — and that love deserves more than a photo left on a phone. A handmade keepsake brings their memory into the real world, where you can see it and hold it.

Crafted From Your Pet's Own Photos

Slow, deliberate, entirely by hand.

Again Paws specializes in handcrafted pet keepsakes and memorials. Every piece is carefully handmade based on your pet's photos, capturing unique details, personality and emotions.

Each order is a quiet collaboration: you send photos, we shape the wool and wood, and together we bring a beloved companion back into view. From a pocket-size mini to a full-body replica, from a garden plaque to a wooden urn — the work is slow, deliberate and made entirely by hand.
