About This Piece

Made from your photos, one at a time.

For the days when words are hard. The Farewell & Remembrance Set gathers the small rituals that help: a hand-poured memorial candle, a printed remembrance card with your pet's photo, a linen scatter pouch for a favourite spot, and a small keepsake token to keep in a pocket or a drawer. Together they make space for a calm, personal goodbye.

Every order starts with a conversation: send your photos, tell us about your pet, and we confirm every detail — size, personalization and finish — before production begins.

Good To Know

Common questions.

Is this suitable as a sympathy gift?

Very much so — it is one of our most-gifted sets. We can ship directly to the recipient with a handwritten note.

Can I order the set without the candle?

Yes, tell us in your inquiry and we will adjust the quote.

How long does the candle burn?

Approximately 20–25 hours, sized for several evenings of remembrance.
