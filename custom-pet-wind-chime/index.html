About This Piece

Made from your photos, one at a time.

Some pets are remembered in sound as much as in sight. This memorial wind chime hangs a wooden tag engraved with your pet's photo, name and dates, above a ring of metal tubes tuned to a soft, low tone. Hang it on a porch, by a window or near a favourite spot in the garden — every breeze becomes a quiet hello.

Every order starts with a conversation: send your photos, tell us about your pet, and we confirm every detail — size, personalisation and finish — before production begins.

Good To Know

Common questions.

Can I put any photo on the tag?

Yes. Send a clear, well-lit photo where your pet's face fills the frame. We confirm the crop with you before engraving.

Can it hang outdoors?

The wood and tubes are finished for outdoor use. Under a porch or eaves the engraved tag will stay crisp for years.

What text fits on the tag?

A name and dates sit comfortably alongside the photo. A short line such as “Always with me” can be added on request.
