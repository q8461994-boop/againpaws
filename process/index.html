Our Process

From a single photo to a finished keepsake.

Every Again Paws piece is shaped slowly by hand. Here is what happens between your photo and the moment it arrives at your door.

A calm, hands-on journey.

1

Send Pet Photos

Share a few clear photos of your pet — front, side and close-up of the face.

2

Confirm Design Details

We agree on size, pose, colours, expression and the frame or base you prefer.

3

Handmade Crafting

Our artisans shape wool, wood and print fibre by fibre until the likeness appears.

4

Quality Check

Every detail is reviewed against your photos before the piece is approved.

5

Delivery Worldwide

Carefully packed and shipped to your door with tracked delivery.

Materials

Soft, natural & made to last.

  • Premium natural wool felt, soft to the touch
  • Warm hardwood and weatherproof finishes for memorial pieces
  • Hypoallergenic filling for safe display at home
  • Optional name engraving and memorial card
  • Secure, gift-ready packaging

Care

Keep the memory beautiful.

  • Dust gently with a soft brush or cool air
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and humidity
  • Store in the included box when not on display
  • Outdoor memorial pieces are finished for year-round weather
