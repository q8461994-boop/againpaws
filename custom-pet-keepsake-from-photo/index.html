Custom pet keepsake made from your photo

A handmade custom pet keepsake turns the details you love—expression, markings and personality—into something you can hold and keep.

A keepsake that begins with the photos you already have

A favorite photo can hold a pet’s familiar look and character. When you share a clear face photo plus another angle or detail image, it gives the maker a stronger reference for a one-of-a-kind needle-felted keepsake.

For a gift, a memory or everyday display

Custom pet keepsakes are meaningful for pet parents, families and friends who want a personal present. Tell us whether your piece is for a gift or remembrance and include the details that matter most to you.

How to request your piece

  1. Choose your favorite pet photos.
  2. Send them with the style you prefer.
  3. Share your U.S. destination for a shipping quote.
  4. Receive a personal quote before the handmade work begins.
