How a photo becomes a felted keepsake

A custom pet keepsake made from a photo is not printed or molded — it is built by hand, one poke at a time, with a barbed needle that tangles wool fiber into firm sculpture. Your photos are the blueprint: we match the fur color, the white socks, the crooked ear, the exact way your pet tilts its head.

The four steps

  • You send 2–5 clear photos (see our photo guide) and tell us the pose you want.
  • We reply within 24 hours with a quote, a timeline and honest advice on what we can capture.
  • A studio artist needle-felts your piece over 2–4 weeks, sending progress photos on larger works.
  • Your keepsake ships worldwide in protective packaging, with tracking.

What it costs and what is guaranteed

Pieces range from $87 for a Mini Portrait to $477 for a Full-Body portrait. Every order is covered by our stage-based refund policy and a damage-in-transit guarantee — details on the products page and in the FAQ.
