About This Piece

Ready to send, made to last.

A place to visit. Each memorial stone is cast with a built-in photo window and a small planter top, so you can set your pet's picture and a few fresh flowers into the same spot. Good To Know

Common questions.

Is the stone personalised?

No — these are ready-designed stones with four fixed inscriptions. You add your own photo to the built-in window, and we ship straight away.

Can it stay outside all year?

Yes. The stone is cast and sealed for outdoor use. In hard frost, bring it under cover to protect the photo print.

What size photo fits?

A standard small print trimmed to roughly 6 × 4 cm sits behind the window.
