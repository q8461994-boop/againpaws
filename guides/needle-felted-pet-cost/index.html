Custom pet pricing guide

How much does a custom needle-felted pet cost?

At Again Paws, handmade custom needle-felted pet pieces range from $87 to $477. The final quote depends on the style, size and visual detail of your requested piece. Shipping is quoted separately.

Why custom work has a range

A handmade piece is priced for the amount of work needed to recreate your pet. A smaller or simpler keepsake needs a different level of work from a more detailed portrait or full-body piece. Clear reference photos also help us understand your request before quoting.

How to receive an accurate quote

  • Send your favorite pet photos.
  • Tell us which product style you like.
  • Share your destination ZIP code for shipping.
  • Tell us if the piece is for a gift or a special date.

What is included in the quote?

Your quote covers the custom handmade item. Because shipping is not included in the product price, we calculate it separately using your destination. This gives you a clear total before work begins.

Ready to start your custom piece?

Send your pet's photos, preferred style and U.S. destination. We will reply with a personal quote; prices range from $87 to $477, with shipping quoted separately.
