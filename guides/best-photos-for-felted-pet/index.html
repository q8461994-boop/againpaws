Photo guide

What photos are best for a custom felted pet replica?

The best photos show the features you most want remembered. You do not need professional photos—clear images from your phone can be a wonderful reference.

Start with these three photos

  1. Face photo: a bright, in-focus image where the eyes, nose and markings are visible.
  2. Side or body photo: useful for coat color, ear shape and posture.
  3. Favorite expression: the photo that feels most like your pet.

Helpful details to mention

Tell us your pet's name, any distinctive markings, the expression you love most, and whether the finished piece is a memorial or a gift. If a detail is important to you, include a close-up photo where possible.

Photos do not need to be perfect

Older photos can still be meaningful. Send the clearest images you have, and include more than one angle when possible. We will use them to understand the details of your custom request.
