How to turn your pet photos into a custom needle-felted replica

A needle-felted pet replica is a one-of-a-kind wool keepsake made by hand from photos of your real pet. It can celebrate a companion who is still by your side or preserve a meaningful memory.

What makes a photo-based replica personal?

Each pet has details that matter: coat markings, eye color, ear shape, favorite expression and posture. Reference photos give the maker a visual guide for those features, so the finished piece reflects your pet rather than a generic animal.

What to send when you request a quote

  1. A clear, well-lit front-facing photo.
  2. A side or full-body photo when you want body shape or markings included.
  3. Your preferred keepsake style or size.
  4. Your U.S. city, state and ZIP code so shipping can be quoted accurately.

What happens next?

After you send your photos, Again Paws discusses the piece and provides a personal quote. Custom needle-felted pet pieces start at $87 and range up to $477 depending on the selected style and detail. Production usually takes 2–4 weeks; shipping is quoted separately.
