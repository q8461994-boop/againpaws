Good To Know

Common questions.

What exactly is in the box?

Treats (tuna, salmon, wagyu beef and M9 steak), pet milk, biscuits, freeze-dried snacks, grooming items, a full set of traditional paper offerings with folded ingots, a memorial letter, an incense burner and a keepsake card — 55 pieces in total.

Is this a religious set?

The paper offerings follow a traditional East Asian farewell custom. Many families use the whole set; others use only the treats and keepsakes.

Can it be shipped as a gift?

Yes. Tell us in the order notes and we will leave the price out of the parcel.
