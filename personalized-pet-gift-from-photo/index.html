Why a personalized pet gift beats anything store-bought

A mug with a paw print is nice. A sculpture of “the actual dog” — same ears, same markings, same goofy grin — makes people cry happy tears when they unwrap it. That is what a personalized pet gift made from a photo does that nothing off the shelf can.

Perfect for every occasion

  • Birthdays and holidays — for the person whose phone gallery is 90% pet photos.
  • New-puppy and adopt-a-versary gifts — celebrate the newest family member.
  • Memorial keepsakes — a gentle way to honor a pet who has passed.
  • “Just because” — honestly, most of our orders are this one.

Ordering is simple

Pick a style on the products page (from $87), send us the photos, and we reply within 24 hours with a quote and timeline. Production takes 2–4 weeks, ships worldwide with tracking, and every detail is confirmed with you before felting begins — see the FAQ for the full picture.
